30-Day Master Teaching Curriculum Roadmap

A day-by-day lesson plan segregating all 20 platform portals into a 6-week intensive boot-camp. Includes learning objectives, real IT scenarios, hands-on labs, and interview questions for every single day.

Week 1: Mathematical Foundations & Python Internals (Days 1–5)

DAY 01
MATH

Linear Algebra from Scratch — Scalars, Vectors & Matrices

🔗 Open Page 0: Math Foundations →
🎯 What to Teach: Explain scalars vs vectors vs matrices. Step-by-step dot product calculation $A \cdot B = \sum a_i b_i$ and matrix multiplication.
🎬 Real IT Scenario: Word embeddings in AI (e.g. measuring cosine similarity between 'King' - 'Man' + 'Woman' = 'Queen').
DAY 02
MATH

Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors & Principal Component Analysis (PCA)

🔗 Open Page 0: Eigenvalues Section →
🎯 What to Teach: Formula $Av = \lambda v$. How eigenvectors capture maximum variance axis for dimensionality reduction.
🎬 Real IT Scenario: Facial recognition (Fisherfaces / Eigenfaces) compressing 1,000D pixel arrays to 50D.
DAY 03
CALCULUS

Calculus for ML — Derivatives, Partial Derivatives & Gradient Descent

🔗 Open Interactive Parabola Sandbox →
🎯 What to Teach: Derivatives as slope of tangent line. Chain rule $\frac{dz}{dx} = \frac{dz}{dg} \cdot \frac{dg}{dx}$. Gradient update $w = w - \alpha \nabla f$.
🎬 Real IT Scenario: Optimizing neural network loss functions without exploding or vanishing gradients.
DAY 04
STATS

Statistics & Bayes' Theorem Deep Dive

🔗 Open Probability & Bayes Section →
🎯 What to Teach: Mean, Median, Standard Deviation, Gaussian Bell Curve (68-95-99.7 Rule), Bayes' Theorem $P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)}$.
🎬 Real IT Scenario: Naive Bayes Email Spam Filter & Medical Diagnostic False Positive Paradox.
DAY 05
PYTHON

CPython Internals — Small Int Caching, GIL & Advanced OOP

🔗 Open Page 11: Python Teaching Codex →
🎯 What to Teach: Why integers [-5 to 256] are cached, GIL thread locking, Descriptors, Metaclasses, Exception handling & Decorators.
🎬 Real IT Scenario: Infosys / TCS backend optimization: avoiding memory bloat in high-throughput Django/Flask services.
