Case Study 1 · Streaming & Video

🎬 Netflix: The 2-Stage Multi-Armed Recommender System

How Netflix filters 50,000+ catalog titles down to a personalized Top 10 in <50 milliseconds.

Architectural Blueprint: Candidate Retrieval ➔ Heavy Re-Ranking

50M QPS · Sub-50ms SLA
[User Action: Login / Scroll] │ ▼ [Stage 1: Candidate Generation (Vector Retrieval)] ├─ User Tower (Deep Neural Net ➔ 128D User Vector) ├─ Video Tower (Transformer Embeddings ➔ 128D Title Vector) └─ Approximate Nearest Neighbors (ANN via ScaNN/HNSW) ➔ Filters 50,000 titles down to 500 candidates (<10ms) │ ▼ [Stage 2: Heavy Ranking & Slate Generation] ├─ Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (LightGBM) + Contextual Features (Device, Time of Day, Network Speed) ├─ Multi-Task Ranking: P(Click) × P(Watch > 15 mins) × (1 - P(Thumbs Down)) └─ Dynamic Artwork Personalization (Contextual Bandits pick thumbnail image) │ ▼ [Personalized Homepage Grid Displayed to User]

🔥 Senior IT Interview Takeaway: In FAANG system design interviews, never propose running a heavy Transformer over the entire catalog of millions of items. Always articulate the Two-Stage Architecture: Fast Vector ANN Retrieval for High Recall ($\approx 500$ items) followed by a Complex GBDT / Cross-Network for High Precision ranking.

Case Study 2 · Mobility & Real-Time Dispatch

🚗 Uber & Ola: Real-Time Surge Pricing & Spatial Matchmaking

How Uber utilizes Uber H3 Hexagonal Spatial Indexing and Reinforcement Learning for dynamic pricing.

[1. Hexagonal Spatial Partitioning (Uber H3 Index)] ├─ World divided into hierarchical hexagons (Resolution 8 $\approx 460$ meters across) └─ Drivers & Riders stream GPS coordinates to Kafka ➔ Flink real-time stream aggregation │ ▼ [2. Supply-Demand Elasticity Engine] ├─ Supply = Count(Available Drivers in H3 Cell + Adjacent Rings in 5-min window) ├─ Demand = Count(App Opens + Ride Requests in H3 Cell in 2-min window) └─ Elasticity Multiplier: $Multiplier = 1.0 + \max\left(0, \gamma \cdot \frac{\text{Demand} - \text{Supply}}{\text{Supply} + \epsilon}\right)$ │ ▼ [3. Global Bipartite Graph Matching (Hungarian Algorithm)] └─ Minimizes total ETA across all ride requests simultaneously rather than greedy 1st-come-1st-served.
Case Study 3 · Food Delivery & Logistics

🍔 Zomato & Swiggy: Multi-Modal Order Delivery ETA Prediction

Breaking down total delivery time into Kitchen Prep (KPT) + First Mile + Last Mile transit times.

$$\text{Total ETA} = \text{KPT (Kitchen Prep Time)} + \text{First Mile (Driver to Rest)} + \text{Last Mile (Rest to Home)}$$ 1. Kitchen Prep Time (KPT) Model: - Features: Restaurant dish count, active kitchen queue size, rain weather flag, historical chef speed. - Algorithm: LightGBM Regressor with Asymmetric Loss (underestimating prep time is 3x more penalized than overestimating). 2. Travel Time Model: - OpenStreetMap OSRM graph + Historical Traffic Speed Embeddings + Live GPS deltas.

Case Study 5 · Audio Intelligence

🎵 Spotify: Discover Weekly Audio Matrix Factorization (ALS)

Combining Collaborative Filtering (Implicit Feedback) with CNN Raw Audio Spectrogram Analysis.

1. Collaborative Filtering (Implicit Feedback ALS): - Interaction Matrix $R_{u,i}$: Plays, skips (negative), replays, playlist saves (strong positive). - Alternating Least Squares (ALS) factorizes $R \approx U \times V^T$ to find latent taste vectors. 2. Audio Content Analysis (Solving Cold Start for New Tracks): - Raw MP3 ➔ Mel-Spectrogram (Time-Frequency image) ➔ Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) ➔ Extracts Tempo, Key, Energy, Danceability vectors.
